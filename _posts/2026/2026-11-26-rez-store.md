## How to Play

Each job can have up to three phases:

1. **Puzzle**: Reassemble torn photo pieces by dragging and dropping them. Click a piece to rotate it 90° clockwise, or right-click to rotate it counterclockwise.
2. **Clean**: Drag the brush across the photo to remove dirt and grime. On some photos, scrubbing too much can damage the print.
3. **Color**: Adjust the temperature and tint sliders to correct the photo's color.

Deliver the photo to receive a grade (S-F) and earn money based on your score. Spend your earnings in the shop on brushes, tools, and other upgrades before starting your next job.

## Features

- 37 photo-repair jobs with increasing difficulty
- Jigsaw puzzles with piece rotation in later jobs
- Brush-based cleaning, with damage from over-scrubbing
- Color temperature and tint correction
- Shop with three brushes, upgrade tiers, an inspection tool, and color sliders
- S-F grading system with score-based payouts
- Game progress is autosaved in your browser
- Relaxing lo-fi soundtrack

## Background

I made this game for the [Cute & Curious Game Jam](https://itch.io/jam/cute-curious-game-jam). Inspired by [PowerWash Simulator](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/PowerWash_Simulator), I wanted to create a management/simulation game focused on cleaning and restoring images. To keep it simple, the game features mechanics such as scrubbing, assembling jigsaw puzzles, and editing colors.

I chose [Phaser](https://phaser.io/) as the game engine and [phaser-jsx]({% post_url 2024/2024-01-08-phaser-jsx %}) as the UI library.

Check out the source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/remarkablegames/rez-store) and give the game a play!
