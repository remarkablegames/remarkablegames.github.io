## How to Play

The ball rolls along a narrow path that zigzags, turning 90 degrees with each line. Tap, click, or press **Space** to turn.

Time your turn just right. Turn too early or too late, and the ball falls off.

Earn 1 point for each line you cross. The ball speeds up as you play and your high score is saved.

## Controls

Turn with:

- Tap or click
- Space, Enter, or ↑
- Gamepad A

## Features

- **Procedurally generated paths**: every run lays down a new staircase of straight segments.
- **Speed that ramps up**: the ball starts at 4.5 units per second and gains speed with every point, up to a cap of 10.
- **Milestones**: every 10 points, the backdrop walks to a new hue, the score pops taller, and a burst of particles celebrates the moment.
- **Animations**: a trail behind the ball, squash-and-stretch on each turn, particle bursts for turning, scoring, and falling, and a camera shake when the ball leaves the path. Camera shake follows your system's reduced motion setting.
- **Sound**: sound effects for turning, scoring, falling, and game over. Plus, lofi music plays in the background.
- **Saves**: your high score is saved to `localStorage`. On Wavedash, there are achievements, a high score leaderboard, and cloud saves.
- **Controls**: mouse, touch, keyboard, and gamepad support.
- **Responsive**: supports desktop and mobile browsers.

## Background

I made **ZAG** for [LittleJS Jam 3](https://itch.io/jam/littlejs-jam). The theme is **ZIGZAG**, and the rules require the game to be made with the [LittleJS Engine](https://github.com/KilledByAPixel/LittleJS) and to be free to play in the browser.

I also participated in the Wavedash challenge by publishing the game on [Wavedash](https://wavedash.com/games/zig-zag) and implementing three features with the SDK that LittleJS's `WavedashPlugin` provides:

1. leaderboards
2. cloud saves
3. achievements

On Wavedash, the game posts a high score to the `high_score` leaderboard. It also saves the high score to a cloud slot so the score follows the player across devices. And it reports the `HIGH_SCORE` and `RUNS_PLAYED` stats so achievements can be unlocked.

Check out the source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/remarkablegames/zag) or play the [game](https://wavedash.com/games/zig-zag).
