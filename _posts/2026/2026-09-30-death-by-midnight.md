> You're a detective hired to read a dead man's will at midnight—but when someone dies, the night resets.
>
> Trapped in an endless cycle, you must uncover the truth, prevent the deaths, and escape the loop.

## Features

- An in-game clock where every action advances time
- Point-and-click exploration
- Characters with branching dialogue
- Inventory items to collect and use
- Multiple endings, including good, bad, and true endings
- Estimated playtime: 15–30 minutes (3,000+ words)

## Warnings

- Jumpscares
- Depictions of death, murder, and suicide
- Blood

## Background

I made **Death by Midnight** for the [Spooktober 8th Annual Visual Novel Jam](https://itch.io/jam/spooktober-2026).

For this jam, I had an idea for a time-loop mystery where a detective's investigation is interrupted every time someone dies.

To support that idea, I added time, exploration, and inventory mechanics to the visual novel. Walking and talking advances the clock, while certain actions unlock new dialogue and story branches.

For the characters, I used an asset pack by [Kalaverita](https://kalaverita.itch.io/), which includes sprites for a butler, nurse, maid, and young miss. The cast fits the game's Victorian setting well.

For the backgrounds, I layered the [mansion pack](https://potat0master.itch.io/free-visual-novel-backgrounds-mansion-pack) with a [tea stained paper texture](https://majcher.itch.io/tea-stained-paper-textures) to create an antiquated look.

I then placed items throughout the different rooms. These items can prevent deaths or unlock new dialogue. To help them fit the environment, I tinted the images to match the evening and nighttime scenes.

Finally, I found several music and sound effects to tie everything together. I chopped the music into loops and converted them to OGG.

From [Love & Terror [BGM Pack - Vol 01]](https://melancholy-marionette.itch.io/love-terror-vol-01-bgm-pack), I used:

- **Fractal Fragments** (95 BPM) for the death scenes
- **Misery** (125 BPM) for the true ending scene

From [Free music pack](https://sinetient.itch.io/free-music-pack), I used:

- **Stone Walls** (87 BPM) for the menu, intro, and ending scenes

Before releasing the game, I used a [script](https://gist.github.com/remarkablemark/687e0b9df31fd795493fbdd978c68d70) to create an animated cover image.

Check out the source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/remarkablegames/death-by-midnight).
