## Overview

This puzzle game was developed during Global Game Jam 2019 (GGJ), where the theme was:

> **What home means to you**

We chose [Phaser](https://phaser.io/) as our game framework and [matter.js](https://brm.io/matter-js/) as our physics engine. We also scaffolded the project from the [phaser-template](https://github.com/remarkablegames/phaser-template) repository.

The game mechanics were inspired by a [matter.js example](https://labs.phaser.io/view.html?src=src\physics\matterjs\advanced%20shape%20creation.js) found in [Phaser Labs](https://labs.phaser.io/).

Our goals while building the game were to keep it _**accessible**_, _**responsive**_, and _**intuitive**_—there are no instructions, so players learn by experimenting. We aimed to keep everything as simple as possible: the only way to interact with the world is by clicking or tapping. Currently, the game has three levels.

Possible improvements include:
- improving the physics,
- integrating sound,
- enhancing gameplay,
- adding transitions and animations,
- and refining the UI.

Overall, this was an amazing game jam. Thanks to everyone who made it possible—and to the playtesters who stopped by during the showcase. It warmed our hearts to hear that the game was _"fun"_ and _"addicting"_.
